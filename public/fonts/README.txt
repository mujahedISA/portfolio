Fonts bundled with this site
============================

Press Start 2P  — by CodeMan38
VT323           — by Peter Hull

Both are published on Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which
permits bundling and redistributing the font files with a website. Full licence
text: https://openfontlicense.org

They are served from this folder rather than from Google's CDN on purpose: it is
faster, it keeps the site working offline, and it means no visitor's browser
makes a request to a third party just to read the page.
